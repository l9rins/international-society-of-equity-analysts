Accessibility Statement

Commitment and conformance standards ensuring digital accessibility for all users across ISEA platforms.

This document forms part of the official governance framework of the International Society of Equity Analysts (ISEA) and should be read together with the related documents listed above.

The International Society of Equity Analysts ("ISEA", "we", "us" or "our") is committed to making its website accessible and usable by as many people as reasonably practicable, including people with disability.

ISEA recognises that accessibility is an important part of providing an inclusive and professional online environment for its members, applicants, analysts, employers, educators, researchers and members of the public.

This Accessibility Statement explains ISEA's approach to accessibility and how users can report accessibility difficulties.

Our Commitment

ISEA seeks to design, develop and maintain its website so that it is:

accessible to people with different abilities;

reasonably easy to navigate and understand;

compatible with commonly used assistive technologies;

usable across a range of devices and screen sizes; and

consistent with recognised accessibility principles and standards.

ISEA aims, where reasonably practicable, to progressively improve the accessibility of its website and digital services.

Accessibility Standard

ISEA aims to align the Website, where reasonably practicable, with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

ISEA's accessibility objective is to work towards WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance.

ISEA recognises that accessibility is an ongoing process and that full conformance may not always be immediately achievable across every page, document, application or third-party service.

Where accessibility limitations are identified, ISEA will seek to address them having regard to their significance, available resources, technical feasibility and the nature of the affected service.

Accessible Website Features

Depending on the particular page and functionality, ISEA seeks to provide features such as:

logical page structures;

descriptive headings;

meaningful link text;

keyboard-accessible navigation;

appropriate text alternatives for meaningful images;

readable typography;

sufficient visual distinction between content and backgrounds;

responsive layouts for different screen sizes;

accessible forms;

clear instructions and error messages;

captions or transcripts for relevant multimedia where reasonably practicable; and

compatibility with commonly used browsers and assistive technologies.

Images and Alternative Text

ISEA seeks to provide alternative text for images where appropriate.

Alternative text may describe the purpose or relevant content of an image so that users who cannot see the image can understand its intended meaning.

Decorative images may not contain alternative text where doing so would unnecessarily interfere with assistive technology.

Documents and Downloadable Content

ISEA may make documents available through the Website, including:

membership materials;

professional publications;

research reports;

educational materials;

application forms;

policies;

event materials; and

other documents.

ISEA will seek to provide accessible versions of documents where reasonably practicable.

However, some historical, third-party or technically complex documents may not fully comply with applicable accessibility standards.

If you require an accessible version of a document, please contact ISEA using the contact details below.

Video and Multimedia

Where reasonably practicable, ISEA seeks to provide accessibility features for multimedia content, including:

captions;

transcripts;

descriptive information; and

accessible controls.

The availability of these features may vary depending on the source and format of the content.

Keyboard Access

ISEA seeks to ensure that core Website functionality can be accessed using a keyboard without requiring a mouse or other pointing device.

Users who experience difficulty navigating the Website using a keyboard are encouraged to contact ISEA.

Screen Readers and Assistive Technologies

ISEA seeks to design the Website to work with commonly used assistive technologies, including screen readers and browser accessibility features.

Compatibility may vary depending on:

the browser;

operating system;

assistive technology;

device;

Website functionality; and

third-party services.

ISEA cannot guarantee compatibility with every assistive technology or configuration.

Third-Party Services

The Website may incorporate or link to services operated by third parties, including:

payment processors;

membership management platforms;

event registration systems;

video platforms;

social media platforms;

authentication services;

analytics services; and

other external services.

ISEA does not control the accessibility of third-party platforms.

Where practical, ISEA seeks to select third-party services that provide reasonable accessibility.

If you encounter an accessibility barrier caused by a third-party service, please contact ISEA so that we can consider whether an alternative method of accessing the relevant service can be provided.

Accessibility of Membership and Application Services

ISEA seeks to ensure that important membership-related processes are accessible, including where reasonably practicable:

membership applications;

registration processes;

account management;

event registration;

payment processes;

professional development activities; and

access to member information.

If you are unable to complete an online process because of an accessibility barrier, please contact ISEA.

ISEA will consider reasonable alternative methods of completing the relevant process.

Requesting an Accessible Format

If you require information published on the Website in an alternative or more accessible format, you may contact ISEA.

Depending on the circumstances, ISEA may be able to provide information in an alternative format such as:

accessible HTML;

accessible PDF;

Word document;

plain text;

large-print format; or

another reasonably practicable format.

Requests will be considered on a case-by-case basis.

Reporting an Accessibility Barrier

ISEA welcomes feedback concerning the accessibility of its Website.

If you encounter an accessibility problem, please contact us and, where possible, provide:

the webpage or feature involved;

a description of the accessibility problem;

the device and browser being used;

any assistive technology being used; and

the outcome you were attempting to achieve.

This information will assist ISEA in identifying and addressing the issue.

Accessibility Feedback

Accessibility feedback may be submitted to:

International Society of Equity Analysts

Email: info@equityanalysts.org

Website: www.equityanalysts.org

Subject: Website Accessibility Feedback

ISEA welcomes both positive feedback and reports of accessibility difficulties.

Reasonable Assistance

Where a person experiences an accessibility barrier that prevents them from accessing an important ISEA service or information, ISEA will seek to provide reasonable assistance or an alternative method of access where reasonably practicable.

The availability of an alternative method may depend upon:

the nature of the request;

the particular service involved;

technical limitations;

the format of the information;

the resources reasonably available to ISEA; and

the requirements of applicable law.

Accessibility and Inclusion

ISEA is committed to promoting an inclusive professional environment.

Accessibility is considered as part of ISEA's broader commitment to:

professional inclusion;

equal participation;

respect and dignity;

diversity;

professional development; and

equitable access to information and opportunities.

Limitations

Although ISEA seeks to make its Website accessible, it is not possible to guarantee that every page, document, feature or third-party service will always be fully accessible.

Some accessibility limitations may arise from:

legacy content;

third-party platforms;

externally supplied material;

technical limitations;

browser or operating-system differences;

newly introduced Website features; or

content that has not yet been remediated.

ISEA will seek to address material accessibility issues as they are identified.

Continuous Improvement

Website accessibility is an ongoing process.

ISEA may periodically:

review Website accessibility;

test Website functionality;

assess identified accessibility barriers;

improve Website design and code;

update documents;

improve accessibility guidance;

consider user feedback; and

implement appropriate accessibility improvements.

ISEA may also update this Accessibility Statement to reflect changes to its Website, accessibility practices or applicable standards.

Changes to This Statement

ISEA may update this Accessibility Statement from time to time.

The current version will be published on the Website and will include an updated "Last Updated" date.
