Introduction

The International Society of Equity Analysts ("ISEA", "we", "us or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of personal information entrusted to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how ISEA collects, holds, uses, discloses and protects personal information in connection with:

the ISEA website;

ISEA membership;

membership applications;

professional designations and post-nominals;

examinations and assessments;

continuing professional development;

events and conferences;

publications and research;

mentoring programs;

employment and career services;

communications and marketing;

online accounts; and

other ISEA activities and services.

This Privacy Policy applies to individuals who interact with ISEA, including prospective and current Members, Fellows, students, applicants, candidates, authors, researchers, event participants, mentors, employers, job applicants and Website visitors.

This Privacy Policy is intended to explain ISEA's privacy practices in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles ("APPs"), to the extent those laws apply to ISEA.

ISEA will review this Privacy Policy periodically and update it where reasonably necessary to reflect changes in our practices, technology, services or applicable privacy requirements.

Who We Are

The entity responsible for the management of personal information covered by this Privacy Policy is:

International Society of Equity Analysts (ISEA)

Address: Head Office, Level 18, 324 Queen Street, Brisbane, Queensland, Australia 4000

Email: info@equityanalysts.org

Website: www.equityanalysts.org

For privacy enquiries, please contact ISEA using the contact details specified in section 18.

What is Personal Information?

"Personal information" generally means information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable, whether the information or opinion is recorded in material form or not.

Personal information may include information such as your name, contact details, professional history, qualifications, membership status, employment information, payment information and information about your interactions with ISEA.

Some personal information may constitute sensitive information under Australian privacy law. Sensitive information is subject to additional protections under the Privacy Act.

What Personal Information Do We Collect?

Depending on your relationship with ISEA, we may collect some or all of the following categories of information.

4.1 Identity information

This may include:

full name;

preferred name;

date of birth where required;

photograph;

signature;

nationality or citizenship where relevant to an application;

identification information where reasonably required for verification; and

membership or candidate identification numbers.

4.2 Contact information

This may include:

residential or business address;

postal address;

email address;

telephone number;

professional website;

LinkedIn or other professional profile information.

4.3 Professional information

This may include:

employer;

job title;

professional history;

years of experience;

professional qualifications;

academic qualifications;

professional memberships;

licences or registrations;

areas of professional expertise;

professional references;

CVs and resumes; and

information relevant to eligibility for ISEA membership or professional designations.

4.4 Membership information

This may include:

membership category;

membership history;

application information;

membership status;

designation status;

post-nominal eligibility;

disciplinary or professional conduct information where relevant;

CPD records;

examination or assessment information; and

communications with ISEA.

4.5 Payment information

Where you make a payment to ISEA, we may collect information necessary to process or reconcile that payment.

Payment card details may be processed by our third-party payment service providers rather than being stored directly by ISEA.

4.6 Event and program information

If you register for an ISEA event, conference, webinar, course, mentoring program or other activity, we may collect:

registration information;

attendance information;

dietary requirements;

accessibility requirements;

professional interests;

participation records; and

photographs, video or audio recordings where applicable.

4.7 Research and publication information

If you submit research, articles, papers, presentations or other material to ISEA, we may collect:

your name;

professional biography;

qualifications;

employer;

contact information;

research information;

author information; and

other information associated with the submission.

4.8 Communications

We may retain information contained in communications between you and ISEA, including:

emails;

enquiries;

complaints;

feedback;

correspondence;

telephone records where lawfully recorded; and

other communications.

4.9 Website and technical information

When you use our Website, we may collect technical information such as:

IP address;

browser type;

device type;

operating system;

pages visited;

approximate location derived from technical information;

referral information;

session information;

Website interaction information; and

information collected through cookies and similar technologies.

4.10 Information supplied by third parties

In some circumstances, we may receive personal information from third parties, such as:

employers;

professional organisations;

educational institutions;

referees;

event partners;

payment providers;

technology providers;

publicly available professional profiles; or

other persons who are authorised to provide information to us.

Where we collect personal information about an individual from another person or entity, APP 5 may require us to take reasonable steps to ensure the individual is aware of specified matters concerning the collection.

Sensitive Information

ISEA generally seeks to minimise the collection of sensitive information.

Where sensitive information is reasonably necessary for an ISEA function or activity, ISEA will handle that information in accordance with applicable privacy law.

Examples may include information relating to:

health or accessibility requirements for an event;

disability accommodations;

professional conduct matters where legally relevant; or

other categories of sensitive information recognised under Australian privacy law.

Where required by law, ISEA will obtain consent before collecting sensitive information.

You should not provide sensitive information to ISEA unless it is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose or ISEA has requested it.

How We Collect Personal Information

We may collect personal information:

6.1 Directly from you

For example, when you:

apply for membership;

create an account;

register for an event;

purchase an ISEA service;

apply for a designation;

submit research;

subscribe to communications;

contact us;

participate in a survey; or

otherwise provide information to us.

6.2 Automatically

We may collect certain technical information automatically when you use the Website through cookies, server logs, analytics tools and similar technologies.

6.3 From third parties

We may collect information from third parties where reasonably necessary and permitted by law.

6.4 From publicly available sources

Where permitted by law, we may collect professional information from publicly available sources, including professional websites and professional networking platforms.

6.5 Unsolicited information

If ISEA receives unsolicited personal information, we will assess whether we could have collected that information lawfully under APP 3. Where required by APP 4, we will destroy or de-identify unsolicited personal information that could not have been lawfully collected.

7.4 Education and CPD

evaluating programs.

7.5 Events

registering attendees;

administering events;

facilitating networking;

issuing event communications; and

7.6 Publications and research

evaluating submissions;

communicating with authors;

publishing approved content;

administering research programs.

7.7 Career services

Where applicable, ISEA may use personal information to:

administer job-board services;

7.8 Communications

We may use personal information to communicate with you regarding:

membership;

professional opportunities;

educational programs;

7.9 Administration and operations

We may use personal information to:

administer our organisation;

maintain records;

maintain security;

improve our services;

conduct internal analysis; and

comply with legal obligations.

Marketing and Direct Communications

ISEA may send you information about ISEA activities, events, publications, membership opportunities, professional development and other services that may be relevant to you.

Where required by law, ISEA will obtain consent before sending commercial electronic communications.

You may unsubscribe from marketing communications by using the unsubscribe facility included in the communication or by contacting ISEA.

You may continue to receive essential administrative communications, such as membership notices, transactional communications, security notices or legally required communications, even if you unsubscribe from marketing communications.

Disclosure of Personal Information

ISEA may disclose personal information where reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Recipients may include:

9.1 Service providers

Including providers of:

website hosting;

cloud storage;

database services;

payment processing;

email;

communications;

cybersecurity;

analytics;

event management;

membership administration;

examination administration; and

IT support.

9.2 Professional and program partners

Where reasonably necessary to administer a particular ISEA program, event, examination, publication or professional service.

9.3 Employers and professional organisations

Where relevant to a particular service and permitted by law.

9.4 Regulators and authorities

ISEA may disclose personal information where required or authorised by law, including to courts, tribunals, regulators, law enforcement bodies or government authorities.

9.5 Professional verification

ISEA may disclose limited information concerning a person's membership or designation status where necessary to verify an ISEA credential.

9.6 With your consent

ISEA may disclose information where you have consented to the disclosure.

Overseas Disclosure

ISEA operates in an international professional environment and may use service providers or engage organisations located outside Australia.

Accordingly, personal information may be disclosed to or accessed by recipients located overseas.

Depending on the services and technology used by ISEA, overseas recipients may be located in countries including, but not necessarily limited to:

the United States;

the United Kingdom;

Singapore;

the Philippines;

countries within the European Economic Area; and

other countries in which ISEA's service providers operate.

The actual countries to which personal information is disclosed may change as ISEA's technology providers and service arrangements change.

Where required by the Privacy Act, ISEA will take reasonable steps in relation to overseas disclosures, including addressing applicable obligations under APP 8.

ISEA will not state that an overseas disclosure is covered by a particular contractual or legal mechanism unless that mechanism is actually in place.

Storage and Security

ISEA takes reasonable steps to protect personal information from:

misuse;

interference;

loss;

unauthorised access;

modification; and

unauthorised disclosure.

Security measures may include:

access controls;

authentication;

encryption where appropriate;

secure hosting;

backups;

monitoring;

staff or contractor access controls; and

other technical and organisational safeguards.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of any account credentials issued to you.

Retention and Destruction

ISEA will retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including where necessary to:

administer membership;

maintain professional records;

comply with legal obligations;

resolve disputes;

enforce agreements;

maintain financial records;

protect ISEA's legitimate interests; or

satisfy regulatory or professional requirements.

When personal information is no longer required, ISEA will take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify it, unless retention is required or authorised by law.

Certain professional records, membership records and historical designation records may need to be retained for extended periods to maintain the integrity and verifiability of professional credentials.

Cookies and Analytics

ISEA may use cookies and similar technologies to:

operate the Website;

maintain security;

remember preferences;

analyse Website usage;

improve Website performance; and

understand how users interact with our Website.

Further information is provided in the ISEA Cookie Policy.

Your browser may allow you to control or disable certain cookies. Some Website functions may not operate correctly if certain cookies are disabled.

Third-Party Websites

The ISEA Website may contain links to third-party websites.

ISEA is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites.

You should review the privacy policy of any third-party website before providing personal information.

Access to Personal Information

Subject to applicable exceptions, you may request access to personal information that ISEA holds about you.

To request access, contact ISEA using the details in section 18.

ISEA may need to verify your identity before processing an access request.

ISEA will generally respond to a valid access request within the period required by applicable law.

In some circumstances, access may be refused where permitted or required by law. If access is refused, ISEA will generally provide reasons for the refusal unless an exception applies.

Correction of Personal Information

ISEA takes reasonable steps to ensure that personal information it holds is accurate, up-to-date, complete and relevant for its intended purpose.

If you believe that information ISEA holds about you is inaccurate, incomplete, out-of-date, irrelevant or misleading, you may request that it be corrected.

ISEA will investigate reasonable correction requests and take appropriate action where required.

Anonymity and Pseudonyms

Where practicable, ISEA will allow individuals to interact with it anonymously or using a pseudonym.

However, anonymity or pseudonymity may not be practicable for activities such as:

membership applications;

professional designation applications;

examinations;

credential verification;

payment transactions;

events requiring registration;

disciplinary matters; or

circumstances where ISEA is required or authorised by law to identify an individual.

This reflects the operation of APP 2, which generally provides individuals with the option of dealing anonymously or pseudonymously, subject to specified exceptions.

Privacy Enquiries and Complaints

If you have a question, concern or complaint regarding ISEA's handling of personal information, please contact:

Privacy Officer

International Society of Equity Analysts

Email: info@equityanalysts.org

Postal Address: Head Office, Level 18, 324 Queen Street, Brisbane, Queensland, Australia 4000

A privacy complaint should, where possible, include:

your name;

contact details;

the nature of the complaint;

relevant dates;

relevant correspondence; and

the outcome you are seeking.

ISEA will investigate privacy complaints and respond within a reasonable period.

ISEA will endeavour to resolve privacy complaints directly with the complainant.

If you are dissatisfied with ISEA's response, or ISEA does not respond within the applicable period, you may be able to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Data Breaches

ISEA maintains procedures for responding to suspected or actual data breaches involving personal information.

Where the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme applies and an eligible data breach occurs, ISEA will comply with its obligations under the Privacy Act, including notifying affected individuals and the OAIC where required.

ISEA may also take other appropriate steps to contain, investigate and remediate a data breach.

Children and Minors

ISEA's services are principally directed toward adults and professionals.

Where ISEA knowingly collects personal information from a minor, it will handle that information in accordance with applicable privacy law and any additional safeguards considered appropriate in the circumstances.

Where consent is required, ISEA will take reasonable steps to ensure that consent is appropriately obtained.

International Members and Users

ISEA is an international professional society and may have Members and users located in jurisdictions outside Australia.

Australian privacy law may apply to ISEA's handling of personal information even where an individual is located outside Australia, depending on the circumstances.

Individuals outside Australia may also have privacy rights under the laws of their own jurisdiction.

Where legally required, ISEA will take reasonable steps to comply with applicable additional privacy obligations.

Privacy and Professional Records

ISEA may maintain records concerning professional membership and credentials.

Where reasonably necessary to protect the integrity of an ISEA credential, ISEA may publish or disclose limited information such as:

name;

membership category;

membership status;

designation;

designation status;

date of admission;

expiry or renewal status; and

other information necessary for credential verification.

ISEA will seek to limit public disclosure to information reasonably necessary for the relevant professional purpose.

Photography, Audio and Video

ISEA may photograph, record or livestream certain events, conferences, ceremonies and other activities.

Images or recordings may be used for purposes including:

event documentation;

archival purposes;

reporting;

professional communications;

Website content;

publications; and

promotional activities.

Where required or appropriate, ISEA will provide notice concerning photography or recording and will seek consent where required.

If you have a particular concern about being photographed or recorded, you should contact ISEA before or during the relevant event.

Publication of Member Information

ISEA may maintain public directories or registers of Members, Fellows or designation holders.

Depending on the relevant membership category and applicable policy, information published may include:

name;

professional designation;

membership category;

professional title;

employer;

geographic location;

areas of professional expertise; and

other professional information.

ISEA will not publish personal information publicly merely because it has been collected, and will seek to ensure that public disclosure is consistent with the relevant purpose, applicable consent and privacy law.

Automated Decision-Making

ISEA may use automated systems or software to assist with administrative functions such as:

fraud detection;

security;

communications;

scheduling;

data analysis;

membership administration; or

Website functionality.

ISEA does not currently intend for automated systems to make final decisions concerning a person's professional membership or designation without appropriate human oversight.

ISEA will update this Privacy Policy if its use of automated decision-making changes in a manner requiring additional disclosure under applicable law.
