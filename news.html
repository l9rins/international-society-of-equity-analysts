News & Advocacy

Institutional Voice, Measured Authority

Formal statements, regulatory engagement and considered commentary – issued only where the Society has a substantive position to communicate.

Formal Statements

Press Room & Media Statements

The Society’s Press Room provides a framework for publishing accurate, attributable information about the institution’s positions, activities and formal statements. Media statements are intended to be issued sparingly and only where the Society has a substantive position to communicate, such as a formal response to regulatory developments, a statement on matters affecting the integrity of equity research, or clarification where public commentary has misrepresented its policies.

Statements issued through the Press Room are intended to reflect positions formally adopted by the Governing Council or the relevant Global Standing Committee. The Press Room may maintain a chronological archive of formal statements, written with the same precision the Society expects of its members’ own research — precise, evidenced and free of promotional framing.

Policy Engagement

Regulatory Consultations & Policy Submissions

The Society provides a framework for participating in regulatory consultations and preparing policy submissions on matters bearing on equity research, market conduct and analyst independence. Where supervisory bodies seek input on such matters, the Society may consider a formal submission appropriate where the matter falls within genuine institutional expertise.

Submissions may be prepared under the oversight of the relevant Global Standing Committee, drawing on the practical experience of senior Fellows and external academic affiliates. The Society’s positions are guided by evidence and professional standards rather than considerations of convenience to its membership. Where the evidence supports a position that increases compliance burden, the Society will seek to maintain that position regardless of its reception.

As the Society’s global chapter structure develops, the scope of its regulatory engagement may extend across a range of jurisdictions, reflecting the position that consistent professional standards require engagement wherever equity markets are regulated.

Analysis & Commentary

The Society Insights Blog

The Society Insights Blog provides a platform for timely analysis and professional commentary that may not require the formality of a journal article or white paper. Contributions may be made by members, Fellows, committee representatives and academic affiliates, and are expected to meet a standard of analytical rigour and professional quality consistent with the Society’s broader publications.

Content may cover sector-specific valuation questions, regulatory developments, observations from regional markets and considered commentary on issues and debates within the profession. Contributors are encouraged to express informed positions and substantive analysis rather than simply summarising prevailing consensus.

Media Relations

Media Contact & Expert Commentators

The Media Contact function provides a central point of contact for journalists and other media representatives seeking information or expert commentary from the Society. Enquiries may be directed to appropriate Fellows or senior Members with relevant expertise in areas such as sector analysis, regulatory developments, valuation methodology and market structure.

Expert Commentators may be invited to contribute where their demonstrated expertise and experience are relevant to a particular enquiry. Commentators are expected to distinguish clearly between their personal analytical views and any formal position adopted by the Society, and to communicate complex matters with clarity, accuracy and professional restraint.

The Society does not comment on speculative matters, unresolved internal proceedings, or questions concerning individual members’ conduct while under disciplinary review.

Official Press Statement FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Establishment of the International Society of Equity Analysts (ISEA)

Secretariat Release • Brisbane, Australia / Global • 1 July 2025

The International Society of Equity Analysts (ISEA) today formally announces its establishment as a dedicated international professional association for equity research analysts, valuation specialists, and portfolio managers.

Formed in response to increasing regulatory scrutiny, cross-border jurisdictional divergence, and the imperative for verifiable analytical discipline, the Society establishes a universal professional standard anchored by the Certified Equity Analyst (CEA) qualification and the ISEA Code of Ethics and Standards of Professional Conduct.

“Equity research is fundamentally a fiduciary and market-integrity function. The ISEA provides the global institutional framework, rigorous assessment benchmarks, and independent governance necessary to elevate and safeguard the profession worldwide.”
— Secretariat, International Society of Equity Analysts

Initial governance has been instituted under the Governing Council and Global Standing Committees, overseeing professional ethics, curriculum certification, research objectivity, and candidate assessment across international member chapters.
