Events & Global Forums

Where the Profession's Hardest Questions Are Argued

Conferences, speaker series, symposia and briefings designed for genuine analytical engagement – not passive attendance.

Flagship Event

Annual Global Equity Conference

The Annual Global Equity Conference will provide a focal point for the Society's priorities and professional standards. It is intended to bring together practitioners, academics, regulators and other professionals to examine questions in equity analysis - methodological, regulatory and ethical - that benefit from rigorous discussion and informed disagreement.

Session design will reflect this purpose, with speakers invited to present substantive positions and engage directly with questions and challenges from experienced practitioners and other delegates. The audience will be deliberately composed, bringing together Fellows, institutional investors, regulators, academic affiliates and senior chapter representatives from across the Society's global footprint.

Conference participation will provide an opportunity for members to undertake relevant professional development and engage with contemporary issues affecting the equity analysis profession. Details of the conference, including its location, theme, programme and registration arrangements, will be announced in advance of each annual cycle, with members in good standing receiving priority access where applicable.

Expert Voices

Distinguished Speaker Series

The Distinguished Speaker Series is built on a simple premise: the people worth hearing from are not always the most visible in financial media. Speakers are chosen for demonstrated influence on the discipline itself – through original research, regulatory stewardship, or sustained institutional judgement.

Members are expected to arrive with prepared, substantive questions, and speakers are selected in part for a demonstrated willingness to be pressed on their reasoning. Subject matter ranges across monetary policy transmission, research independence, market microstructure, and governance failures.

Regional Focus

Regional Symposia & Roundtables

Regional Symposia & Roundtables address the fact that equity analysis is practised differently depending on where it is conducted. These formats are deliberately local – grounded in disclosure regimes, sector concentrations and supervisory environments specific to a given chapter's market.

Roundtables operate at smaller scale and greater depth: a limited group of senior practitioners examining one defined question closely enough to produce findings the Society can draw on more broadly. Participants are expected to review background material in advance and contribute substantively.

Timely Response

Webinars & Virtual Briefings

Some developments in equity markets do not wait for the next scheduled conference. Webinars & Virtual Briefings address exactly that category of urgency – regulatory change, accounting standard shifts, sector-specific events with cross-jurisdictional implications.

Sessions are led by Global Standing Committee members, senior Fellows, or external specialists. The virtual format reaches the full international membership without favouring any region – scheduling rotates and sessions are recorded for equitable access across time zones.

Partnership

Sponsorship & Exhibition Opportunities

Sponsorship of a Society event confers association with the profession's global standard-setting body. The Society applies to prospective sponsors the same selectivity it applies to its own institutional partnerships. The separation between sponsorship and content is absolute – no sponsorship tier extends to influence over programme content, speaker selection or analytical positions.

Opportunities are concentrated around the Annual Global Equity Conference and select regional symposia. Exhibition space is reserved for organisations whose offering bears direct relevance to equity analysis: data and research platforms, credentialing technology, and institutional service providers.
