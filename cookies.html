Cookie Policy

Policy explaining the use of cookies, tracking technologies, and user consent choices on ISEA platforms.

Official Policy Record

This Cookie Policy explains how the International Society of Equity Analysts ("ISEA", "we", "us" or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit or use the ISEA website (the "Website").

This Cookie Policy should be read together with the Privacy Policy and Website Terms of Use.

ISEA is committed to maintaining a professional, transparent and responsible approach to the collection and use of information. Cookies and similar technologies may be used to support Website functionality, security, performance, analytics and, where applicable, communications and marketing. The Australian privacy regulator, the OAIC, recognises that cookies and tracking technologies can involve the collection of information that may constitute personal information depending on the circumstances.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files or similar data files that may be stored on your computer, smartphone, tablet or other device when you visit a website.

Cookies allow a website to recognise a particular device or browser and can perform functions such as:

remembering preferences;

maintaining login or session information;

enabling website functionality;

improving website performance;

understanding how visitors use the Website;

detecting security threats or unusual activity; and

measuring the effectiveness of communications and marketing.

Cookies may be session cookies, which generally expire when you close your browser, or persistent cookies, which remain on your device for a specified period.

Types of Cookies We May Use

ISEA may use the following categories of cookies and similar technologies.

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the Website to function properly.

They may be used to:

maintain secure sessions;

authenticate users;

remember necessary security settings;

facilitate membership or account functionality;

process forms;

maintain website security; and

provide core Website functionality.

Because these cookies are necessary for the operation of the Website, disabling them may cause certain Website features to become unavailable or operate incorrectly.

2.2 Functional Cookies

Functional cookies allow the Website to remember choices and preferences made by visitors.

For example, they may allow the Website to remember:

language preferences;

regional settings;

display preferences;

previously selected Website settings; and

other preferences necessary to provide a more personalised experience.

2.3 Analytics and Performance Cookies

ISEA may use analytics technologies to understand how visitors use the Website.

These technologies may provide information such as:

pages visited;

approximate duration of visits;

navigation paths;

referring websites;

browser and device type;

operating system;

approximate geographic information;

Website errors;

Website performance; and

aggregated usage statistics.

ISEA may use this information to improve the Website, identify technical problems, understand visitor behaviour and improve the effectiveness of its services.

Where practicable, ISEA will configure analytics technologies to minimise the collection of information that is not reasonably necessary for these purposes.

The OAIC specifically recognises that analytics tools and tracking technologies may collect information such as IP addresses, pages visited, URLs and session information and recommends appropriate consideration of privacy obligations when deploying such technologies.

2.4 Security and Fraud-Prevention Technologies

ISEA may use cookies, pixels, scripts or similar technologies to:

detect malicious activity;

protect accounts;

prevent fraud;

identify automated or abusive activity;

protect payment and membership systems;

monitor Website security; and

maintain the integrity of the Website.

These technologies may operate in conjunction with third-party service providers.

2.5 Marketing and Communications Technologies

Where applicable, ISEA may use cookies, tracking pixels or similar technologies to understand the effectiveness of communications, campaigns and promotional activities.

For example, these technologies may help ISEA determine whether an email or online campaign has been opened, viewed or interacted with.

Where ISEA uses tracking technologies for direct marketing purposes, it will do so subject to applicable privacy and direct marketing requirements.

The OAIC notes that organisations using tracking pixels for targeted online marketing should provide appropriate transparency and a simple means of opting out where required.

First-Party and Third-Party Cookies

3.1 First-Party Cookies

First-party cookies are placed by ISEA or by technology operating on behalf of ISEA.

They are generally used to provide Website functionality, security, analytics and other services directly associated with the Website.

3.2 Third-Party Cookies

Some Website functions may be provided by third-party service providers.

Those providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies when their services are integrated into the Website.

Third parties may include providers of:

Website analytics;

payment processing;

authentication;

cloud hosting;

security;

embedded content;

communications;

social media functionality; and

other Website services.

Third-party providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

ISEA does not control the privacy practices of independent third parties.

Cookies Used for Payment Services

Where the Website provides access to membership payments, event payments or other transactions, payment service providers may use cookies and similar technologies necessary to:

process transactions;

maintain secure payment sessions;

detect fraudulent transactions;

authenticate transactions;

protect payment information; and

comply with security requirements.

ISEA does not ordinarily receive or store complete payment card numbers where payment processing is performed by an independent payment service provider.

Information relating to payment processing may nevertheless be exchanged between ISEA and the relevant payment provider as necessary to process the transaction.

Information That May Be Collected

Depending on the cookies and technologies deployed on the Website, information collected may include:

IP address;

device information;

browser type;

operating system;

language settings;

approximate geographic location;

pages visited;

links clicked;

referring website;

date and time of visits;

session information;

Website interactions;

device identifiers;

membership or account-related technical information; and

information concerning Website performance.

Not all of this information will necessarily constitute personal information under Australian law.

ISEA will handle personal information in accordance with its Privacy Policy and applicable Australian privacy laws.

Cookies and Personal Information

A cookie does not necessarily identify an individual by name.

However, information collected through cookies or similar technologies may constitute personal information where it can reasonably be associated with an identifiable individual, particularly where technical information is combined with information such as a name, email address or account information.

ISEA therefore takes a cautious approach to the management of information collected through cookies and similar technologies.

The ISEA Privacy Policy provides further information regarding the collection, use, disclosure, storage and protection of personal information.

Your Cookie Choices

Where the Website provides a cookie preference or consent mechanism, you may be able to:

accept all cookies;

reject non-essential cookies;

select particular categories of cookies; or

change your preferences at a later time.

You may also control cookies through your web browser.

Most browsers allow you to:

view stored cookies;

delete cookies;

block cookies;

restrict third-party cookies; or

receive warnings before cookies are stored.

The precise method varies between browsers and devices.

Please note that disabling or blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality, security or performance of the Website.

Cookie Consent

Where consent is required by applicable law, ISEA will seek appropriate consent before deploying cookies or similar technologies requiring consent.

Consent may be obtained through a cookie banner, preference centre or other appropriate mechanism.

Where consent is not legally required, ISEA may use cookies where reasonably necessary to provide the Website or perform the relevant function, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

Withdrawing Consent

Where ISEA relies on your consent to use particular cookies or similar technologies, you may withdraw your consent by using the Website's cookie preference mechanism, where available.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before consent was withdrawn.

Some strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled through the cookie preference mechanism because doing so may prevent the Website from functioning correctly.

Third-Party Analytics

ISEA may engage third-party analytics providers to assist in understanding Website usage and performance.

These providers may collect information concerning your interaction with the Website through cookies, pixels, scripts or similar technologies.

ISEA may configure such services to reduce the collection of unnecessary information and to support the privacy objectives described in this Policy.

Where third-party analytics providers process personal information, their handling of that information may also be governed by their own privacy policies.

Tracking Pixels and Similar Technologies

ISEA may use technologies that perform functions similar to cookies, including:

tracking pixels;

web beacons;

tags;

scripts;

software development kits;

local storage technologies; and

other device or browser identifiers.

These technologies may be used for Website analytics, security, communications, performance measurement and other legitimate business purposes.

ISEA recognises that tracking pixels can potentially collect information including IP addresses, URLs, device information, pages visited and interaction data. The OAIC recommends that organisations carefully assess such technologies and adopt appropriate data-minimisation and transparency measures.

Social Media and Embedded Content

The Website may contain links to or embedded content from third-party platforms, including social media, video, professional networking or other online services.

When you interact with such content, the relevant third party may place cookies or collect information in accordance with its own privacy practices.

ISEA does not control the operation of third-party cookies once you interact with an external service.

You should review the relevant third party's privacy and cookie policies before using those services.

International Service Providers

Some third-party technology and service providers used by ISEA may be located outside Australia or may process information outside Australia.

Accordingly, information collected through cookies or similar technologies may potentially be processed in countries other than Australia.

Further information regarding overseas disclosures of personal information is provided in the ISEA Privacy Policy.

Data Security

ISEA takes reasonable steps to protect information collected through the Website against:

misuse;

interference;

loss;

unauthorised access;

modification; and

unauthorised disclosure.

However, no internet transmission, Website or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Retention of Cookie Information

ISEA will retain cookie-related information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or as otherwise required or permitted by law.

The retention period may vary depending upon:

the type of cookie;

its purpose;

whether it is a session or persistent cookie;

the relevant third-party provider; and

applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

Changes to This Cookie Policy

ISEA may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect:

changes to the Website;

changes to technologies used by ISEA;

changes to third-party service providers;

changes to privacy practices;

changes to applicable laws or regulatory guidance; or

other operational or organisational changes.

The updated version will be published on the Website with a revised "Last Updated" date.

Relationship With the ISEA Privacy Policy

This Cookie Policy explains specifically how ISEA uses cookies and similar technologies.

The ISEA Privacy Policy provides broader information concerning:

personal information collected by ISEA;

how personal information is used;

disclosure of personal information;

overseas disclosures;

information security;

retention;

access and correction;

privacy complaints; and

other rights and obligations under applicable privacy laws.

Where information collected through cookies constitutes personal information, the Privacy Policy applies in addition to this Cookie Policy.

Contact US

If you have questions regarding this Cookie Policy or ISEA's use of cookies and similar technologies, you may contact ISEA using the contact details published on the Website.

International Society of Equity Analysts

Email: info@equityanalysts.org

Website: www.equityanalysts.org

Privacy Complaints

If you have a concern about ISEA's handling of personal information collected through cookies or similar technologies, you may first contact ISEA so that the matter can be investigated and addressed.

ISEA's Privacy Policy contains the applicable privacy complaint procedure.

Nothing in this Cookie Policy limits any rights you may have under applicable Australian privacy legislation.
