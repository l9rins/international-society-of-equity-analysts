Professional Development

Development That Strengthens Judgement

Continuing professional development is not a compliance formality – it is the ongoing obligation of every serious equity analyst.

Continuous Learning

CPD Learning Portal

The CPD Learning Portal is intended to provide Members with structured access to professional development content spanning valuation methodology, sector-specific analysis, regulatory developments, ethics case studies and emerging analytical techniques. As the Society develops its CPD framework, content may be developed or reviewed with appropriate input from the Global Standing Committees to ensure that it reflects genuine advancement in professional practice.

The Portal is designed around depth rather than volume. Rather than focusing on the accumulation of hours through passive video consumption, the CPD framework is intended to encourage engagement with material requiring genuine analytical thought, including case-based ethics modules, technical sessions involving worked application, and assessments designed to verify comprehension rather than attendance.

Compliance with applicable CPD requirements is intended to form part of the requirements for maintaining post-nominal rights. The Society may maintain an auditable record of completed CPD activity, with appropriate records available where necessary for regulatory or employer confirmation of ongoing professional competence.

Advanced Learning

Advanced Masterclasses & Workshops

The Advanced Masterclasses & Workshops program is designed for practitioners who have moved beyond foundational competence. Masterclasses are intended to be narrow in scope, with each session examining a discrete analytical challenge in depth, such as advanced valuation techniques for complex capital structures, sector-specific modelling or emerging disclosure standards.

Workshops are intended to complement Masterclasses through case-based exercises in which participants can work through practical analytical problems with appropriate expert guidance. Sessions may be structured around smaller groups and, where appropriate, restricted to Members and Fellows, reflecting the level of prior competence that advanced material may assume.

The program is intended to emphasise depth, practical application and analytical judgement rather than broad introductory coverage.

Senior Leadership

Executive Education Programs

Executive Education Programs are intended for senior analysts, portfolio managers and research leaders whose professional development needs differ from those earlier in their careers. Programs may address the management of research teams while preserving analytical independence, navigating institutional conflicts, engaging with regulators and boards, and exercising professional judgement where evidence is incomplete.

The Executive Education framework may encompass intensive short-form programs and longer executive courses, with delivery potentially undertaken in collaboration with appropriately qualified academic institutions or other educational partners. Certain programs may include a formal assessment or credential recognised by the Society, subject to the applicable program requirements and governance arrangements.

The programs are intended to provide structured opportunities for experienced practitioners to deepen their professional judgement, leadership capabilities and understanding of the institutional responsibilities associated with senior analytical roles.

Professional Guidance

Mentorship & Leadership Programs

Mentorship & Leadership Programs are intended to support the development of Members and emerging practitioners through structured opportunities for professional guidance, leadership development and engagement with the Society.

The Mentorship Program may facilitate appropriate connections between Fellows or senior Members and Associates or early-career Members, with potential matching based on factors such as sector specialisation, regional market exposure and individual development objectives. Mentors may provide guidance on analytical judgement, career development, professional conduct and the practical responsibilities associated with a career in equity analysis.

Leadership Programs are intended to provide a framework for Members seeking to develop the capabilities required for future roles within the Society, including chapter leadership, committee service and potential consideration for senior governance responsibilities.

Both programs are founded on the principle that professional standards are strengthened through the deliberate transmission of knowledge, experience and professional judgement between generations of practitioners.

Market Intelligence

Technical Briefings & Regulatory Updates

Technical Briefings & Regulatory Updates are intended to provide Members with structured access to analysis and commentary on significant regulatory and market developments. Rather than following a fixed academic schedule, briefings may be convened in response to developments warranting focused professional consideration, including changes to disclosure regimes, research objectivity requirements, market conduct supervision, and structural regulatory developments affecting particular sectors or regions.

Regulatory Updates may be maintained as a reference resource, enabling Members to follow the development of significant regulatory matters over time. Where the Society adopts an institutional position or interpretation, briefings may examine how relevant developments intersect with the Society’s Research Objectivity Standards and Best Practice Guidelines.

The program is intended to provide timely, substantive analysis of developments that may affect the practice and professional responsibilities of equity analysts.
